### Write your novel. It stays yours.

A free, local-first writing app for novelists, with no account, no upload and no server, so your words stay on your own computer.

[**Download**](https://github.com/capactiyvirus/inkgrove/releases/latest) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Try it in your browser](https://inkgrove.app) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [**Join the Discord**](https://inkgrove.app/discord)

</div>

## What you get

- **A writing space built for books**, with chapters and scenes in a binder you can drag around, focus and typewriter modes, daily word goals, spellcheck that learns your character names, and `[[links]]` between scenes and notes.
- **A story bible beside the manuscript**, with character sheets you can shape to your own template, world entries, research notes, and a corkboard and plot grid for planning threads across scenes.
- **History that keeps every version**, so you can compare any two, find a line you cut weeks ago, name the moments that matter, and restore without losing what you had.
- **Your files, not ours**, kept in a library file on your computer with automatic backups and a Markdown copy of each book, and exports to Word, EPUB, PDF, HTML and Markdown whenever you want them.
- **Easy to move in**, importing Word, Markdown, plain text, RTF and Scrivener projects, with one long document split into chapters by its headings.
- **Works offline and needs no account**, and anonymous crash reports and usage counts never include your writing and can each be switched off in Settings.

## Download

Windows, macOS and now **Linux** are all on the [latest release](https://github.com/capactiyvirus/inkgrove/releases/latest).

| | File | First run |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Inkgrove_<version>_x64-setup.exe` | SmartScreen: **More info**, then **Run anyway** |
| **macOS** | `Inkgrove_<version>_universal.dmg` | **System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway** |
| **Linux** (new) | `Inkgrove_<version>_amd64.AppImage` | `chmod +x` the file, then open it (newer Ubuntu also needs `sudo apt install libfuse2t64`) |

The app is not code-signed yet, so the first-run warning is expected. After that, updates install from inside the app.
